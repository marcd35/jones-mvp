# Education and jobs reference

**Status: draft for author review.** T-02 / issue #4. This reference uses the V1 r4 spec in this change, based on r3 at `edebd52` plus T-01 fieldwork and the author's decisions in this session (2026-11-02). Observed facts and v1 defaults are distinguished below. T-10 (#28) and T-11 (#31) remain blocked until the author approves this reference.

## Author decisions and source priority

- T-01's fresh-game observations are authoritative when they differ from the earlier design document.
- Where T-01 did not establish a value, the earlier design document may supply a **replaceable v1 default**. Those values are assumptions, not observations from the original game.
- A successful Work action adds one experience point to the Player's current career. Experience is retained within that career. This is the approved v1 rule; the original game's accumulation rate was not visible in fieldwork.
- V1 has no automatic firing rule. A clothing refusal does not end the held job.
- The original-game fieldwork confirms that worn/torn clothes refuse Work with no pay and no Time spent, and that replacing clothes restores Work. The author decided that v1 has no rung-specific minimum clothing tier. Worn/torn clothes still block Work at every job, and a refusal does not end employment.

## Education path

V1 has a starter level and one further level. Higher courses and job rungs are displayed as locked when their requirements are not met.

| V1 level | Courses and status | Evidence and default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — no course | No education credential | V1 data default. |
| 1 — starter | Trade School or Jr. College | T-01 observed both on the initial course list. Trade School was completed; Jr. College was not. |
| 2 — one deeper step | A technical or academic course after the starter | T-01 showed Electronics and Pre-Engineer. after Trade School but did not take either. The design document suggests Electronics/Pre-Engineer on the technical path and Bus. Admin/Academic on the college path. These are replaceable defaults, not confirmed course rules. |
| Above level 2 | Locked in v1 | The design document's Engineering, Grad School and later courses are outside the one-deeper-step scope. Show them as locked only if represented in the data. |

T-01 observed that Trade School takes 10 classes, each costing about 9.5% of a Week and no additional money. The enrollment fee was $50 in Week 1 and $52 in Week 3 and followed the price level. Finishing Trade School added it to the Statistics education list. These are observations for Trade School only; they do not establish later courses' fees, class counts, Time costs or effects. T-11 must keep those values in data and label any defaults it supplies.

### Education terms used by the design defaults

To map the design document's broad labels to the three v1 levels, use these replaceable defaults:

- `None`: level 0.
- `Trade School`, `Trade School+`, and `Basic education`: level 1 (or above for `+`).
- `Jr. College` and `College education`: level 1 or above on the college path.
- `Technical education`, `Technical training`, `Basic technical skills`, and `Engineering degree`: level 2 on the technical path.
- `Advanced education` and `Advanced degree`: level 2 on either path.

The design document does not define exact course equivalencies or numeric cutoffs. T-10 and T-11 must represent the approved levels in data and keep the mapping replaceable.

## Experience and job retention

For v1, each successful Work action adds one point to the Player's experience for that career. The current job identifies the career; T-10 must give jobs a data-defined career ID so that several rungs in the same career share experience. Switching careers does not erase previously earned experience in another career. The initial default for a rung marked `Experience` or `Management experience` is at least one point in that career; the threshold can be tuned in data.

The original fieldwork did not reveal an experience counter after about 40 Cook shifts. It did verify that Factory Assembly Worker required experience even after Trade School was completed. The rule above is the author's v1 default, not a claim about how the original game counts experience.

V1 does not fire a Player automatically. T-01's earlier “work habits” warning and inferred firing were not reproduced; the Cook remained employed through Week 9. A worn-clothing refusal gives no pay and spends no Time, and it does not end employment. Missing work, warnings, quitting and any future firing behavior are outside this reference unless a later decision adds them.

## Data-pack mapping

T-03's `Job` type currently has `id`, `title`, base hourly `wage`, employer `locationId`, numeric `educationRequired`, `shiftHours`, `workTimeHours`, and optional `clothingRequired`. It does not yet represent experience or career identity.

T-10 should extend the data model with a career ID and an experience threshold for each rung. The Player state should retain experience by career so switching jobs does not discard it. The current-price job listing should scale the base wage with the price level; hiring snapshots that displayed wage as the held wage. Omit `clothingRequired` for v1; the author decided there is no per-rung minimum clothing tier. Keep the worn/torn-clothing refusal as a global Work rule. T-11 should put course names, prerequisites, fees, class counts and Time costs in education data, not rules code. The rules should continue to consume the data pack rather than hard-code employers, courses, wages or requirements (DEC-007 / AC-014).
