# Spec: V1 / r3

Source requirements and decision record: the author's decisions in the scoping session of 2026-11-01 (summarised in the DEC table below); repository `marcd35/jones-mvp` at `8fff73c` (branch `docs/design-docs`, PR #2); `docs/design/game-design.md` and `docs/README.md` at that revision; `docs/research/original-game-fieldwork.md` (working tree, uncommitted: rounds 1 and 2 plus verification notes); `CONTEXT.md` (working tree, glossary).

Readiness: **draft, no open decisions**. OD-1 to OD-4 were settled in r3 (DEC-023 to DEC-026). The remaining unknowns (UNK-002 to UNK-010) affect economy numbers, which the balance pass sets; none blocks a ticket from starting. Changes from r2 and r1 are in the revision log.

Vocabulary follows `CONTEXT.md` (Player, Jones, Week, Turn, Round, Time, Board, Location, Step, Goal, Goal points).

## Objective

Replace the MVP's rules (4 locations, 2 jobs, flat 1-hour travel, one goal) with a first slice of the real game: a walkable 5x5 Board of 13 Locations, with the four-goal points setup, a hidden inflation economy, jobs, education, food, clothing and rent. A new Player can play from setup to a win or a loss in one sitting, on desktop or on a phone, on a shared Vercel link. v1 is for the author and friends and family, and is built so the author can extend it by editing data.

Observable outcome: the six "done" criteria (AC-015, AC-017 to AC-021) all pass, and AC-022 and AC-023 hold before the first shared link.

- DEC-017 and DEC-021 (see the table).

- A fixed seed is available for every random choice, so runs are repeatable. Source: needed for AC-010 and AC-018; inferred.
- Wealth counts cash plus goods unless the original says otherwise. Source: design doc "Accumulate $2000 in cash/assets"; to be confirmed in UNK-005.
